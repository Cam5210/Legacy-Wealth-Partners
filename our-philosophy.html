Our AI Philosophy & Case Studies

How we approach AI adoption across product development, engineering, sales, and operations

Our Core AI Philosophy

We view AI as a human enablement tool—not a replacement. Our approach focuses on designing for the future rather than just solving today's problems.

For Product Teams

Our philosophy: Enable product teams to transition from ideation to MVP to market at unprecedented speed. We're reimagining the traditional product development workflow.

Traditional Approach

1

Product Team Ideation

Product team generates initial idea

2

Create Mockups

Creates Figma or PowerPoint mockups

3

Hand-off to Engineering

Sends to SWE team with explanations

4

SWE Builds MVP

Engineering team builds first version (MVP)

5

Multiple Iteration Cycles

Product team reviews, requests changes, repeated back-and-forth cycles

6

User Testing

Limited testing with actual users

7

SWE Team Builds Full Version

SWE team builds and deploys the final version of the feature

8

Repeat steps 5-7 multiple times

Every time the product team wants a change they ask the SWE team to build the change

9

Feature is Accepted

After multiple iterations the product feature is finally accepted

10

Generate Revenue

The feature is finally able to go into production and start generating revenue

Problems:

  • Slow, labor-intensive process
  • Costly resource investment
  • Delayed market entry
  • Vision lost in communication
  • Engineers working on unproven ideas
  • Harder to preserve product vision

AI-Enabled Approach

1

Product Team Ideation

Product team generates initial idea

2

Product Team Builds MVP with AI

Product team directly creates working MVP using AI tools

3

User Feedback

Gather and analyze real user feedback on working product

4

Repeat Steps 3 & 4

Product team directly refines MVP based on feedback

5

SWE Team Builds Full Version

Engineers build production version of the already validated feature

6

Generate Revenue

Validated ideas are sold to clients and able to generate revenue

Benefits:

  • Faster time-to-market
  • Reduced development costs
  • Product vision preserved
  • Engineers focused on proven ideas
  • Constant user feedback for product validation

Case Study: The Daniel Group

The Daniel Group's product team built and shipped a new feature in under 2 months using this AI-enabled approach. Their team built the MVP, iterated on it based on user feedback, then had the SWE team finalize and ship the product. The feature is already generating new revenue in significantly less time than their traditional development cycle would have allowed.

Benefits:

  • Rapid and Agile: Highly efficient and responsive
  • Cost-Effective: Doesn't need a large team
  • Shorter GTM timelines: Streamlined market entry
  • Preservation of Ideas: Visionaries' ideas aren't lost
  • Product Team Involvement: More active collaboration
  • Enhanced Understanding: Product teams gain insight into SWE challenges
  • Time Optimization: SWE team members freed from unnecessary tasks
Example Success: The Daniel Group successfully ideated, built, and released a new product feature within a mere two months, achieving a remarkable sixfold reduction in their typical timeline.

Benefits:

  • Dramatically reduce time to market for new features and bug fixes
  • Reduce engineering costs
