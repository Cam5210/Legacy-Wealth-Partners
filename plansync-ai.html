About PlanSync AI

AccessibleAI's first product, PlanSync AI, is a AI platform designed to help 401(k) advisors manage plans more efficiently and stay compliant — without the chaos.

PlanSync is a perfect example of what's possible with the right AI strategy.

Built from scratch by the founders of Accessible AI, it reflects everything we teach in our consulting work — that with clarity, speed, and smart use of AI, small teams can solve big, complex problems faster and better than teams 10x the size.

How We Did It

3 Founders + AI

Contrary to popular belief, PlanSync AI wasn't built by a large team or backed by venture capital. It was created entirely by the three co-founders of AccessibleAI — from scratch, in under 8 months, with no outside funding or technical help.

This allowed us to uncover insights others missed, validate ideas quickly, and design a product experience that feels intuitive because it was built around real-world advisor behavior.

Software Engineering

Full-Stack Expertise
