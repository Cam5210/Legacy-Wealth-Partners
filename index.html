Your Options: Monetization Without Giving Up Control

Explore flexible strategies to unlock value from your practice while maintaining the level of control that works for you.

Minority Equity Sale

Pros:
  • Maintain control
  • Immediate liquidity
  • Keep running your practice
Cons:
  • Share future profits
  • Partner input required
  • Less than full value
Real Scenario: Sell 30% of your $2M practice for $600K cash, keep control, and benefit from growth resources.

Full Practice Sale

Pros:
  • Maximum value
  • Clean exit
  • No ongoing obligations
Cons:
  • Loss of control
  • Client transition risk
  • End of income stream
Real Scenario: Sell your entire book for 2.5x revenue, transition clients over 6 months, and retire completely.

Sell & Stay Models

Pros:
  • Liquidity event
  • Continue working
  • Reduced admin burden
Cons:
  • Employment terms
  • Performance targets
  • Less autonomy
Real Scenario: Sell practice for $3M, sign 5-year employment agreement, earn 40% payout on revenue.

Revenue Participation Without Equity

Pros:
  • Keep ownership
  • Access resources
  • Flexible terms
Cons:
  • Share revenue
  • No upfront cash
  • Ongoing obligations
Real Scenario: Partner with platform, share 15% revenue for tech/support, scale practice 2x in 3 years.
