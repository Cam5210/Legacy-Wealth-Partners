About Cameron

Cameron Abernethy is the co-founder and CEO of AccessibleAI, and the founder of PlanSync AI — an AI-powered platform designed to streamline complex workflows through automation and clarity.

A self-taught software engineer and product strategist, Cameron brings deep hands-on experience across AI, product development, and full-stack execution. He built PlanSync AI from the ground up with his co-founder in under 8 months — entirely bootstrapped, with no outside funding — by applying the exact AI-driven frameworks that now power AccessibleAI's consulting work.

Cameron's approach is grounded in real-world results: start lean, solve meaningful problems, and leverage AI at every step to move faster and build smarter. Today, he helps companies apply the same principles to unlock efficiency, sharpen product direction, and bring truly intelligent tools to life.

"AI isn't magic — it's leverage. When you apply it with focus, even small teams can solve massive problems faster than most companies ever will."
